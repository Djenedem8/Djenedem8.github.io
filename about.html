Organic cotton, Mud textile About Our Fashion Store

Bogola – In the Mud" is a clothing focused company that produces and tailors clothing using the Bogolanfini cloth. It is a means of sharing and exposing the Malian culture to the rest of the world by way of fashion. The founder, Dienebou Sacko is someone who takes pride in her culture and took it upon herself to create a business that would not only expose her culture but also hopefully inspire a new generation of creators. Bogola – In the Mud celebrates Malian culture by blending modern fashion trends with cultural tradition and builds a workplace where each employee feels valued and acknowledged. Essentially Bogola – In the Mud is a brand that allows for artisans from across the globe to put their own creative touches into each piece. .

We guarantee that you will fall in lust with our decadent blends the moment you walk inside until you finish your last sip. Join us for your daily routine, an outing with friends, or simply just to enjoy some alone time.
